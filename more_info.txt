# SMARTRESTAURANT OS — FULL USER FLOW EXPLANATION

## How the system works for:

# 1. Restaurant Owners

# 2. Customers

This is the practical operational journey from setup to daily use.

---

# PART A — RESTAURANT OWNER FLOW

# “How a restaurant joins and uses the system”

---

# STEP 1: RESTAURANT REGISTRATION

The restaurant owner creates an account on SmartRestaurant OS.

## They provide:

* Restaurant name
* Location
* Contact details
* Business type (fast-food, premium, hotel, etc.)
* Opening hours
* Logo/images

---

# STEP 2: DIGITAL RESTAURANT DASHBOARD CREATION

Once registered, the system automatically creates:

## Their own restaurant management dashboard

Think of this like:

### “Your restaurant control center”

---

# STEP 3: MENU SETUP

The owner uploads and organizes their menu.

## They add:

### Categories:

* Starters
* Main dishes
* Drinks
* Desserts

### For each item:

* Name
* Price
* Description
* Photo
* Availability status

---

# STEP 4: TABLE CONFIGURATION

The owner creates their restaurant table structure.

## Example:

* Table 1
* Table 2
* Table 3
* VIP Table A

Each table receives:

# A unique QR code

---

# STEP 5: QR CODE GENERATION

For every table, SmartRestaurant OS generates a unique QR code.

## Important:

This QR code is not generic.
It contains:

### Restaurant ID + Table ID

### Example:

Table 12 at Urban Grill ≠ Table 12 at another restaurant

---

# STEP 6: QR PLACEMENT

The restaurant prints and places QR codes:

* On tables
* Menu cards
* Wall displays
* VIP rooms

---

# STEP 7: DAILY OPERATIONS BEGIN

From this point:
The restaurant can now receive:

* Table orders
* Reservations
* Menu views
* Customer activity

All through one dashboard.

---

# OWNER DASHBOARD FEATURES:

## Live:

* Incoming orders
* Which table ordered
* What was ordered
* Order status
* Reservation requests

---

# STEP 8: ORDER MANAGEMENT

When a customer orders:

### Dashboard receives:

“Table 8 ordered:

* 2 Grilled Chicken
* 1 Coke”

---

# STEP 9: STAFF / KITCHEN EXECUTION

Restaurant staff can:

* Confirm order
* Prepare
* Mark as in progress
* Mark as served

---

# STEP 10: ANALYTICS

Over time, the owner can track:

* Most popular dishes
* Peak hours
* Daily sales trends
* Table activity

---

# RESTAURANT OWNER VALUE:

## They gain:

### More control + Faster service + Better organization + Data

---

# PART B — CUSTOMER FLOW

# “How a customer experiences the system”

---

# SCENARIO:

A customer arrives at a restaurant using SmartRestaurant OS.

---

# STEP 1: CUSTOMER SITS AT TABLE

They sit at:

### Table 12

On the table is:

### A QR code

---

# STEP 2: QR CODE SCAN

Customer scans using smartphone camera.

## No app download required

This is critical.

### Scan → Opens instantly in browser

---

# STEP 3: LANDING PAGE OPENS

The system automatically recognizes:

### Restaurant + Table Number

## Customer sees:

### “Welcome to Urban Grill — Table 12”

---

# STEP 4: DIGITAL MENU ACCESS

Customer browses:

* Meals
* Drinks
* Promotions
* Combos
* Photos
* Prices

---

# STEP 5: ORDER SELECTION

Customer selects desired items.

### Example:

* Burger
* Fries
* Juice

Optional:

* Extra cheese
* No onions
* Spice level

---

# STEP 6: ORDER CONFIRMATION

Customer confirms order.

---

# STEP 7: SYSTEM SUBMISSION

The system sends:

### “Order from Table 12”

Directly to restaurant dashboard.

---

# STEP 8: ORDER TRACKING (Optional Feature)

Customer may see:

* Received
* Preparing
* Ready

---

# STEP 9: FOOD ARRIVES

Staff serves the correct table.

---

# STEP 10: ADDITIONAL INTERACTIONS

Customer may also:

* Reorder
* Call waiter
* Request bill
* Leave review
* Reserve future visit

---

# CUSTOMER VALUE:

## They gain:

### Faster ordering + Less waiting + More control + Clear menu

---

# CUSTOMER SIDE:

### Digital entry point + Table identifier + Ordering system
